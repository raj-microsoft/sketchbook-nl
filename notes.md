# A Sketchbook of the Netherlands

Original captions for an imagined itinerary; not personal travel testimony. Sources are ordered by the authored route, not capture date.

## 01.jpg
What is visible: Windmills beside a reed-lined canal under blue winter sky.
Place: Kinderdijk — The windmill country
Memory / authored caption: The mills stand so quietly you almost forget they were built to work. I would happily lose an afternoon following their reflections.
Drawing caption: A little wind, a lot of sky.

## 02.jpg
What is visible: An aerial view of concentric canals, brick roofs and narrow streets.
Place: Amsterdam — The canal belt
Memory / authored caption: From up here the canals look like a thought someone kept circling back to. Down there, surely, a tiny table is waiting beside the water.
Drawing caption: Follow the water. Get wonderfully lost.

## 03.jpg
What is visible: A narrow canal, waterside houses, trees and an arched footbridge.
Place: Giethoorn — The village on the water
Memory / authored caption: A bridge, a bend, another patch of green. This feels like the sort of place where arriving late might be the whole point.
Drawing caption: No hurry beyond this bridge.

## 04.jpg
What is visible: The Hoofdtoren beside Hoorn harbour, with boats and waterfront buildings.
Place: Hoorn — At the old harbour
Memory / authored caption: The old tower has the best seat in the harbour. I like the little boats around it, looking as though they have just stopped by for a chat.
Drawing caption: Meet me by the tower.

## 05.jpg
What is visible: Spring flowers and trees in the Keukenhof gardens.
Place: Keukenhof — A garden in full colour
Memory / authored caption: I meant to choose a favourite colour. Then another flower bed appeared, and I had to start all over again.
Drawing caption: A page is hardly enough for spring.

## 06.jpg
What is visible: A long causeway carrying a road between two expanses of water.
Place: Afsluitdijk — Between two waters
Memory / authored caption: Here the horizon does most of the talking. The road keeps going, and for once I am perfectly happy not to add anything.
Drawing caption: Blue on both sides.

## 07.jpg
What is visible: Delft rooftops seen from the Nieuwe Kerk toward the Oude Kerk.
Place: Delft — Above the rooftops
Memory / authored caption: All those little roofs, tucked close together. I keep finding another window, another chimney, another reason not to turn the page yet.
Drawing caption: A city collected one roof at a time.

## 08.jpg
What is visible: Traditional buildings and windmills at Zaanse Schans.
Place: Zaanse Schans — Beside the Zaan
Memory / authored caption: The green timber and the water belong together so nicely. A good place to close the notebook, with a little room left for coming back.
Drawing caption: Until the next little adventure.
